FAQ

How do I play?

Your current two-tile hand is shown at the top. Tap the falling tile that completes a sequence (three consecutive numbers in the same suit) or a triplet (three identical tiles) with your hand. A correct hit scores 100 points and gives you a new hand, and every extra hit in a row adds 25 bonus points. Shooting the wrong tile, or letting a tile that would complete your set get away, costs one of your 3 lives.

Where do I change difficulty and hints?

Open Settings from the home screen to choose Relaxed, Standard or Expert and to turn hints and haptics on or off. Settings save automatically. Haptics apply immediately; difficulty and hints apply from the next round.

How do I reset my best score and stats?

Go to Settings, tap "Reset Best Score and Stats" and confirm. This can't be undone.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. The game works offline, has no ads and no in-app purchases, and needs no account.

Where is my data stored?

Your best score, games played and settings stay on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy.

Which languages are supported?

Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English, following your phone's language. Phones set to other languages show Traditional Chinese.

The game freezes or crashes. What now?

Fully close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, email us and describe the steps that led to it.
