隱私權政策 (Privacy Policy)

最後更新日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日 ｜ 適用應用程式：《雀神塔防 (Mahjong Battler TD)》

1. 總則與承諾

感謝您遊玩《雀神塔防 (Mahjong Battler TD)》（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）。我們非常重視您的個人隱私權。本隱私權政策旨在說明當您使用本應用程式時，您的資料如何被儲存與受到保護。

2. 個人資料的收集與使用

本應用程式為單機策略塔防遊戲，不會主動收集、儲存、分享或出售您的任何個人識別資訊（如姓名、電話號碼、實體地址、定位資訊或設備廣告識別碼 IDFA）。您無須註冊任何外部帳號即可完整遊玩本應用程式的所有功能。

3. 本機資料儲存與 iCloud 雲端同步

  • 本機儲存 (Local Storage)：您的遊戲語言設定、最高守城積分、生涯戰績統計、歷史對戰紀錄與進行中的波次存檔，均透過 Apple 標準的 UserDefaults 儲存於您的個人 iOS 裝置本機中。
  • iCloud 雲端備份與同步 (Apple CloudKit & iCloud Key-Value Storage)：若您的 iOS 裝置已登入個人 Apple iCloud 帳號，本應用程式會透過 Apple 官方提供的 CloudKit 私密資料庫 (Private Database) 與 iCloud 鍵值儲存空間 (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore)，自動備份並跨裝置同步您的最高積分、歷史戰績與中途戰局存檔。
  • 資料隔離與安全性：上述雲端存檔資料完全儲存在您個人的 Apple iCloud 私密空間內，受 Apple 安全加密保護。開發者或任何第三方均無法讀取、存取或查看您的 iCloud 資料。若您希望關閉雲端同步，可隨時於 iOS「設定」→「Apple ID」→「iCloud」中關閉本應用程式的 iCloud 權限，或於遊戲內清除戰績與存檔。

4. 第三方服務與廣告追蹤

  • 本應用程式未內嵌任何第三方廣告 SDK，亦不使用任何跨 App 或跨網站追蹤技術（無 IDFA 追蹤）。
  • 本應用程式不包含任何真實金錢賭博、現金投注或虛擬籌碼兌換機制；遊戲中之麻將牌型僅作為奇幻防禦塔之元素合成規則。

5. 兒童隱私權保護

本應用程式適合所有年齡層（App Store 年齡分級 4+），且不會向任何年齡層的使用者（包含 13 歲以下兒童）收集個人識別資料。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026 | Application: Mahjong Battler TD (雀神塔防)

1. Introduction

Thank you for playing Mahjong Battler TD ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how your game data is handled when you use the App.

2. Personal Data Collection

The App is a single-player strategy tower defense game. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or device advertising identifiers/IDFA). No account registration is required to play.

3. Local Storage & Apple iCloud Synchronization

  • Local Storage: Your language preferences, high scores, lifetime defense statistics, match history, and mid-game wave checkpoints are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults.
  • iCloud Sync (CloudKit & Key-Value Storage): If you are signed into iCloud on your iOS device, the App uses Apple's native CloudKit Private Database and iCloud Key-Value Storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to back up and synchronize your game records and saved checkpoints across your own devices.
  • Privacy & Access Control: All iCloud game data is stored strictly inside your personal Apple iCloud account. Neither the developer nor any third party can access, view, or process your personal iCloud data. You can disable iCloud synchronization at any time via your device's iOS Settings or delete your saved records directly within the App.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

  • The App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs or third-party tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The App contains no gambling, real-money wagering, or casino mechanics. Mahjong tile combinations are used purely as crafting recipes for fantasy defense towers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
