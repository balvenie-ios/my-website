FAQ

How do I play?

Each turn you get a hand of cards. Choose cards to swap, then play your hand: the stronger the poker hand, the more damage you deal. Defeat the enemy before your HP runs out. After each win you choose one upgrade to strengthen your run. Win all 4 battles to clear the run.

What are the hand ranks?

From strongest to weakest: Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, One Pair, High Card. A-2-3-4-5 counts as a straight. The in-game Rules screen shows the damage for each hand.

Is my progress saved and synced?

Your in-progress run, stats and settings are saved on your device automatically, and the game works offline. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also synced to your own private iCloud so you can continue on another device signed in to the same Apple ID. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language?

The game supports Traditional Chinese, English, Vietnamese and Thai, and follows your phone's language. To use a different language just for this game, go to iOS Settings > Wild Poker > Language.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings in the game, tap "Clear Records" and confirm. This can't be undone.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. The game works offline, has no ads and no in-app purchases, and needs no account.

The game freezes or crashes. What now?

Fully close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, email us and describe the steps that led to it.
