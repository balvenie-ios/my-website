Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

"Mahjong Sharpshooter" (the "App") is a single-player game that can be played without an internet connection, and we take your privacy seriously.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App requires no sign-up or login and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Network and permissions

The App does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. Its only network connection is the iCloud save described below.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, number of games played, hit count and settings (difficulty, hints, haptics) are stored on your device. Deleting the App removes this data. You can also reset your stats in the App's Settings.

Data stored in your iCloud

If your device is signed in to iCloud, after each finished game the App uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to save that game's result (score, difficulty, hits, max combo, duration and finish time) to your own private iCloud storage. Only your iCloud account can access this data; we cannot read it and do not use it to identify you. It is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the App or resetting your stats does not delete these iCloud records; you can delete the App's data from iCloud storage management in iOS Settings. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the App uploads nothing.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
